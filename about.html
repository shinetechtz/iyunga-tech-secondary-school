About Our School

Learn more about our history, mission, vision, and commitment to technical excellence.

History & Background

Iyunga Technical Secondary School is a premier government technical institution located in Mbeya City, Tanzania. The school is dedicated to equipping students with high-quality science and technical skills required for modern technological advancement and self-reliance.

With well-established engineering workshops, science laboratories, and a modern KOICA-supported digital library, Iyunga continues to train bright minds that excel both academically and practically.

🎯 Our Vision

To be a leading secondary institution in producing highly skilled, innovative, and self-reliant technical and science graduates in Tanzania and beyond.

🚀 Our Mission

To provide high-quality education, practical technical skills, and moral guidance through effective teaching, modern infrastructure, and dedicated staff.
